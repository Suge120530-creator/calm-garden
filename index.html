For grown-ups

How to play

  • Sit together and play in a calm moment, not during an upset. 5 to 10 minutes is plenty.
  • Your child picks how Bunny might feel. Every pick gets a kind answer, because more than one feeling can fit.
  • After a big feeling, breathe along with the balloon. Breathe slowly yourself, and let your child join when they're ready.
  • Ages 3 to 4: read the words aloud and point to the faces. Ages 5 to 6: ask "Has that ever happened to you? How did you feel?"

Privacy

This game collects nothing. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no links out, and it sends nothing over the internet. "Read aloud" uses only voices already on your device; if your device has none, the button hides and you can read the words yourself.

Good to know

Activities adapted from Big Feelings, Little Hearts by Softly Raised. Created with the help of AI and checked by a person. Educational only, not therapy or medical advice. If your child's big feelings are intense, frequent or getting in the way of daily life, talk with your pediatrician.
